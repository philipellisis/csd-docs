Mini or Mini + Mini Expansion?

On its own, the Mini handles the buttons, plunger, two solenoids (life extenders built in), the service board and the expansion boards. Add the Mini Expansion board for the shaker motor, more solenoids, the knocker and the two output board. With it, the Mini can do everything the PinOne Main can.

Which wires do I need?

Almost every part comes with the wires it needs. The exception is the wire from the Mini Expansion board to the life extenders, which is sold separately as the Terminal Connector Wire:

  • 3 terminal wire for each 2 position life extender (outputs 6–7, 8–9 and 10–11)
  • 4 terminal wire for each 3 position life extender (outputs 3–5)

The two solenoids on the Mini itself don't need a life extender or this wire.

Check polarity

The expansion board cables, the ribbon cables and the strobes can all be plugged in backwards. A reversed ribbon cable won't damage anything, but the buttons or lights won't work. Reversed strobes can damage the strobes or the expansion board. Hover over each wire in the diagram for details.

You don't need every part shown. Start with the Mini, then add the parts you want. See DIY Cabinet Options for help choosing parts.
