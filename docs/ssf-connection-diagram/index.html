Good to know
  • The amplifier has the USB sound card built in, so it plugs straight into the PC. You don't need a separate sound card.
  • Connect each speaker with SSF Speaker Wire. Choose the exciter wire length you need, or the Bass Shaker/Sub version for the bass shaker and subwoofer.
  • Backbox speakers aren't sold by CSD. Use your own and connect them to Speaker Left and Speaker Right.

Once everything is connected, set it up in Windows and in your games: see Configuring the sound card, Configuring SSF on Visual Pinball and Configuring for Pinball FX3.
